Shop & Marketplace

Coming soon: a marketplace built around what we actually teach

Every kit and item in our shop starts as a project in a live WhichCraft session. Once members and the public have had a chance to try it, the best-loved projects graduate into work-from-home kits and finished pieces you can buy — and members get the chance to sell their own creations here too.

Notify Me When It Launches
How the marketplace works

From live session to sellable kit

01

We teach it live

Every monthly session is a real test run — members experience the project firsthand, live with a teacher.

02

We track what resonates

We watch interest and requests for each project to see which ones members and the public genuinely want more of.

03

Popular projects become kits

Projects that perform well get turned into work-from-home kits, stocked and sold to members and the public.

04

We keep the shop fresh

We continually reassess what's selling. Kits that slow down get retired to make room for new projects members love.

What you'll find here

Two ways to shop WhichCraft

📦

Work-From-Home Kits

Curated supply kits for the crafts we teach in our live sessions — everything you need to make that month's project (or a past favorite) on your own time, at your own pace.

🖐️

Member Marketplace

Made something you're proud of in a session? WhichCraft members will be able to list their own finished pieces for sale right here — our best sellers may even get featured or turned into an official kit.

Circle members get discounted pricing across everything in the Shop, on top of early access to new kits.
