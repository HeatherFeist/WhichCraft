Group Travel & Paint Retreats

Paint the world's most beautiful places, together

A few times a year, WhichCraft members travel together to a stunning destination for a small-group retreat — painting the live scene, guided step-by-step, with plenty of time to explore, relax, and connect.

Request Retreat Info
What to expect

Part painting workshop, part dream vacation

🖼️

Daily Guided Painting

Set up en plein air in front of the actual view, guided live through each piece — no experience required, all supplies included.

🏡

Small Group, Real Connection

Retreats are capped at a small group size so everyone gets personal attention and it truly feels like traveling with friends.

🍽️

Curated Local Experiences

Beyond painting, enjoy thoughtfully chosen lodging, local meals, and free time to explore each destination.

How retreats work

From interest to easel, in four steps

01

Tell us you're interested

Fill out the form below so we can notify you first when a new retreat opens.

02

Reserve your spot

Members get early access and a reduced deposit to hold their place — spots are limited and go quickly.

03

Prepare together

A pre-trip guide covers packing, supplies (all included), and what to expect each day.

04

Travel & paint

Arrive, unpack, and start painting — the whole trip is planned so all you have to do is create.
