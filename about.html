About WhichCraft

Built on the belief that making things is better together

WhichCraft started with a simple idea: creativity grows when it's shared. What began as a small local painting circle has grown into a community connecting crafters near and far — through monthly live sessions and unforgettable trips around the world.

Meet your teachers

The two of us can't wait to create with you

WhichCraft is taught live by real people who love this stuff as much as you do. Here's who you'll be learning from.

🎨
Heather
Founder & Lead Instructor

For years I taught painting and craft classes in my own studio, watching the same thing happen every time: people would walk in unsure of themselves and walk out proud of what they'd made — and even prouder of the friends they'd made along the way.

WhichCraft is my way of bringing that feeling to more people, wherever they live. I lead our live sessions, host members in my own home for our in-person get-togethers, and get to do what I love most a few times a year: take a small group of members somewhere beautiful and paint it together, in person.

Our values

What we believe about crafting

🌱

All Levels Welcome

You don't need experience or fancy supplies — just curiosity. Every session is taught so a total beginner and a seasoned crafter can both walk away proud.

🤝

Community Over Competition

There's no "wrong" way to make something here. We celebrate progress, encourage questions, and cheer each other on.

🧭

Real, Live Connection

In a world of pre-recorded content, we keep it live and human — real-time teaching, real conversation, real trips together.
