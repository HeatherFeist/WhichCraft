Host a Party

Open your home, bring the group together — and get a gift for it

Just like the home parties you might remember growing up, WhichCraft members can apply to host a craft get-together in their own home (or a favorite local spot). Gather your friends, we'll bring the project and the teaching, and you'll walk away with a free item of your choice from our marketplace.

Apply to Host
How hosting works

It's simple — and it's on us

🏡

You Provide the Space

Host in your home, backyard, or a local spot that fits your group — as few as 4 guests or as many as your space comfortably holds.

🎨

We Bring the Craft

A teacher joins live (in person or by video) to guide your group through that month's project, with supplies arranged ahead of time.

🎁

You Choose Your Gift

As a thank-you for hosting, you'll get to pick a free item from our marketplace — yours to keep, no purchase required.

What to expect

From application to party day

01

Apply below

02

03

Enjoy the party, then pick your free marketplace item as our thank-you for opening your home.

Questions

Hosting FAQ

Do I need to be a member to host?

It helps, but it's not required — first-time hosts are welcome to apply, and it's a great way to try WhichCraft with friends before joining.

What does my free gift cost me?

Nothing — choosing an item from our marketplace is our way of saying thank you for opening your home and bringing your group together.

Do guests need to be members too?

No, but they'll get a taste of what membership is like, and we'll make it easy for anyone who loves it to join afterward.

What if my group is smaller or bigger than the options listed?

No problem — just note it in the craft preference field and we'll work with you on the right fit.
