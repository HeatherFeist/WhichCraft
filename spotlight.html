Spotlight

Your craft, in front of our whole community

Spotlight is how great crafters get discovered. Circle members can submit a video of their own craft or product, our community votes on the ones they'd love to see more of, and standout crafters get invited to demo live on our channel — with the best of the best eventually invited into The Studio.

Submit to Spotlight
How it works

From submission to livestream

01

Circle members submit

Share a link to a video of your craft, product, or idea. Submitting is a perk of Circle membership.

02

We shortlist

Heather & Linda review every submission and choose which ones go up for a community vote — not all submissions make the shortlist.

03

The community votes

Circle members vote on the shortlisted submissions to help us see what the community is most excited about.

04

Standouts get invited

Popular vote, and the fit with our upcoming livestream calendar, both factor into who we invite to demo live — and repeat standouts may be invited into The Studio.

A high vote count highlights community interest — it's one input into who gets invited, alongside production scheduling, not an automatic booking.

Questions

Spotlight FAQ

Do I have to be a Circle member to submit?

Yes — submitting to Spotlight is one of the perks of Circle membership. Spark members are welcome to upgrade anytime.

Will my submission definitely get voted on?

Not automatically. Every submission is reviewed first, and only shortlisted ones go up for a community vote — this keeps Spotlight focused on projects that fit well.

If I win the vote, am I guaranteed a livestream spot?

The vote shows us what the community is most excited about, but final invitations also depend on our livestream production calendar. A strong vote is the best way to get noticed, though.

Can I submit more than once?

Yes — feel free to submit a new craft or project each month.
