Questions

Frequently asked questions

Can I cancel anytime?

Yes. All memberships are month-to-month with no long-term contract. You can cancel anytime from your account and you'll keep access through the end of your current billing period.

Do I need any experience or supplies to start?

Not at all — sessions are taught live and designed for all skill levels. Both plans include that month's project kit, so you'll always have what you need.

What if I miss the live session?

No problem — Circle members get full replay access anytime, and Spark members can view the replay for 30 days after the live session.

Can I switch or upgrade my plan later?

Absolutely. You can upgrade, downgrade, or cancel your plan at any time — just reach out and we'll take care of it, or manage it directly from your Stripe billing portal link (sent in your welcome email).

How do the travel retreats work with membership?

Retreats are a separate trip cost from your monthly membership, but members get early access and member-only pricing. Visit our Travel Retreats page for upcoming destinations.

How do I get invited to The Studio?

Studio isn't something you purchase — it's how we recognize our most active Circle members. Strong Spotlight submissions, hosting a party, and consistent community involvement are all things we look at. There's no fixed checklist — just keep creating and showing up, and we'll notice.
